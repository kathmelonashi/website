The Making Process

About Me

Welcome to my pottery studio. I've been working with clay for many years, finding joy in the tactile process of creation and the meditative practice of throwing on the wheel.

My work is inspired by organic forms, natural textures, and the beauty of imperfection. Each piece is handmade and unique, reflecting the marks of the making process and the character of the materials.

I believe pottery should be functional as well as beautiful—pieces that bring warmth and artistry into daily life.
