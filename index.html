The Making Process

About Me

I fell in love with ceramics and the art of pottery in 2025. It has been one of the most fulfilling creative experiences of my life. Working with clay brings me back to a sense of childhood wonder. Every day, I practice the art of patience, giving each piece the time, care, and intention it needs to become something truly special.

This website is a space to share my work and the pieces that have come from that journey. Sometimes it's hard to let them go, but some are available for purchase. If a particular piece speaks to you, or you're interested in owning one, please feel free to reach out.
